About

Yunshu Chen · PhD candidate, Monash Medical AI

Distilled to run on hospital hardware without a GPU. Three first-author papers under review; one model already in production use.

How I got here

Second track: microscopy

Same question, different scale: what does a cell model actually need to get right?

  • SAM2 memory as a tracking linker

    CHOTA 0.8603

    Beat a trained SOTA associator with no retraining at all, on 6 of 6 fields of view (p < 0.001).

  • LeadCell, a cell foundation model

    8M+ instances

    DINOv3 + UNETR across five modalities, beating four SAM-family baselines. Deployed as a production web tool.

  • Cell size × resolution benchmark

    19 × 9

    19 datasets against 9 frozen models over four perturbation axes, plus a data audit that found duplication and corruption first.

  • Confidence-Adaptive Trimap

    Under review

    The uncertain boundary band comes from the model's own confidence, not a hand-tuned kernel, and it survives domain shift where the fixed kernel does not.

  • Physically-grounded synthesis

    11 priors × 5 domains

    Point processes and imaging physics instead of generative hallucination. A self-review then killed my own framing and relocated the bottleneck.

Also built

  • Genius Labbook A git-backed research logbook CLI. 135 entries across 6 projects: every experiment I have run since March 2026. Open source, MIT.
  • Cell_Collection Dataset curation across 7 sources: 186 public datasets, with a duplication and leakage audit run before anything reaches a benchmark. Open source.
  • Stroke outcome prediction Multimodal CT-perfusion and clinical fusion with treatment-specific heads, +12% accuracy. Apollo Medical Imaging, completed 2025.

Experience

  • As a researcher

    • Three first-author papers under review, all on validation rather than architecture.
    • For coronary IVUS I moved evaluation from pixel overlap to clinical measurement agreement: CCC 0.94 and 0.96 against expert readings, and 84% agreement on the threshold that decides treatment.
    • Pre-training an ultrasound foundation model on 49.6M frames drawn from 13 years of clinical acquisition.
    • I publish what did not work too: five explicitly falsified directions shipped with the tracking paper.

  • As an engineer

    • I came to research from software engineering, and I build for the machine the work has to land on.
    • A distilled segmentation model 2.6× smaller and 3× faster on CPU, so a hospital without GPUs can still run it.
    • A cell analysis platform in daily production use by an industry partner, with LoRA adaptation to a new cell type from 5 to 10 samples.
    • Infrastructure that is not glamorous but decides whether the science holds: versioned data lineage, crash-safe multi-GPU evaluation, and audits that caught duplication and corruption inside widely used public datasets.
