About

Yunshu Chen · PhD candidate, Monash Medical AI

I build medical imaging AI for real clinical use — models that help decide the next step in treatment.

I work on coronary ultrasound and cell imaging: segmentation validated against the decisions expert readers actually make, an ultrasound foundation model built from a decade of clinical acquisition, and a reporting agent where every number comes from a deterministic tool rather than the language model. Distilled to run on hospital hardware without a GPU. Three first-author papers under review; one model already in production use.

Open to research and ML engineering roles, any industry · Melbourne · PhD completing April 2027

How I got here

Second track — microscopy

Same question, different scale: what does a cell model actually need to get right?

  • SAM2 memory as a tracking linker

    CHOTA 0.8603

    Beat a trained SOTA associator with no retraining at all, on 6 of 6 fields of view (p < 0.001).

  • LeadCell — cell foundation model

    8M+ instances

    DINOv3 + UNETR across five modalities, beating four SAM-family baselines. Deployed as a production web tool.

  • Cell size × resolution benchmark

    19 × 9

    19 datasets against 9 frozen models over four perturbation axes — and a data audit that found duplication and corruption first.

  • Confidence-Adaptive Trimap

    IEEE BIBM

    The uncertain boundary band comes from the model's own confidence, not a hand-tuned kernel — and it survives domain shift where the fixed kernel does not.

  • Physically-grounded synthesis

    11 priors × 5 domains

    Point processes and imaging physics instead of generative hallucination. A self-review then killed my own framing and relocated the bottleneck.

Also built
