THE QUESTION

Intravascular ultrasound (IVUS) captures a sequence of cross-sectional images from inside a coronary artery. My work concerns the next steps: outlining the vessel, calculating measurements and presenting the results for a cardiologist to review.

MY CONTRIBUTION

I build the image-processing pipelines, model integrations and review software, including annotation exports and review-time tracking. Cardiology partners provide annotations and clinical judgement; software testing and clinical evaluation are tracked separately.

Measurement model

GeoCat

I developed lumen/EEM segmentation and geometric measurement tools, with editable contours and traceable source frames. Model evaluation includes expert measurement agreement and efficiency studies. The GeoCat manuscript remains under review following the AAAI 2027 rebuttal.

Clinical evaluation ongoing

IVUS Agent

I built a workflow connecting deterministic measurement tools to locally served Qwen for report drafting, with images and measurements available for clinician review. The offline imaging pipeline is tested on Apple Silicon.

Research in progress

IVUS-FM

I build DICOM processing, data provenance and self-supervised learning pipelines for IVUS and IVOCT. Completed work includes an IVOCT LeVJEPA training run and exploratory IVUS encoder comparisons. Further model comparisons and independent downstream evaluation are ongoing.

From imaging to measurement

3D context. Cross-sectional measurements.

The review tools support contour editing, calipers, source-frame navigation and annotation export. Physical lengths and volumes require verified spatial calibration.

Explore the software & evaluation +

The packaged imaging pipeline processed 5,629 frames across two complete pullbacks on an Apple M2 Pro with 16 GB memory in about 51 minutes. Checks covered segmentation, side-branch and calcium inference, 3D exports and geometry measurements. This was an engineering portability test, not clinical validation.

The reporting prototype uses JSON Schema tool contracts, Pydantic state records and Qwen2.5-7B served locally with vLLM. Measurements come from deterministic tools. Live Qwen or VLM inference was not part of the Mac portability test.

Reader tools distinguish original AI outputs from human corrections and retain annotation provenance. Clinician evaluation and independent reference review are ongoing; exploratory encoder results are not presented as established clinical performance.

THE WORK

The main deliverable is LeadCell, cell-segmentation software used at Lead Healthcare. Around it, I work on three practical questions: how to separate cells in an image, how to follow them through a video, and how to evaluate a model when cell size and imaging conditions change.

WHAT I DELIVERED

I developed and packaged cell-analysis software with a React interface and an internal laboratory API. The software is in use at Lead Healthcare. A newer tracking release candidate connects segmentation, temporal association, lineage export and visual review, with regression checks; organoid division and fusion analysis is ongoing.

Related research

Separate projects. Shared questions about cell analysis.

CellTopo: following cell divisions

CellTopo preserves input masks while using independent forward and backward SAM2.1 propagation to score cell links. I evaluated association and lineage reconstruction, with separate studies of branch verification and selective prompting to examine accuracy and computation trade-offs. This work is a research manuscript.

Calibrating input scale

I built a paired benchmark of three frozen segmentation pipelines across eight input scales and 17 tasks from 15 source families. A budgeted calibration study evaluates accuracy, downside risk and computational cost together. The retrospective benchmark and manuscript are research work, not deployment validation.

Further research

Research in progress

Organoids across time

I integrate segmentation and tracking for real organoid videos, preserving overlapping instances and graphs with multiple parents. The workflow exports lineages, evaluation results and visual review material. Division and fusion events remain candidates for review; the latest package is a tested release candidate.

Exploratory research

MitoSync: division-aware tracking

MitoSync studies joint mother–daughter association and optional synthetic-video supervision for cell tracking. I built controlled comparisons to distinguish the effect of association design from generated training data. The research manuscript and method code are available; synthetic supervision has not shown a consistent advantage across controls.

FishScope · zebrafish regeneration analysis

I developed FishScope with regeneration researchers at Monash to turn birefringence images into muscle and injury masks, quantitative measurements and reviewable reports. The workflow combines PyTorch models, an interactive Gradio interface and batch outputs, with uncertainty flags for manual inspection. The first-author paper is accepted at IEEE BIBM 2026.
